## Presenter Quick-Start Checklist

1. **Launch Services Locally**:
   * Frontend: `http://localhost:3000` (React 18 + Vite)
   * Backend: `http://localhost:8080` (Spring Boot 3)
   * AI Service: `http://localhost:8000` (FastAPI Python)
2. **Open Slides**:
   * Open `JiraPilot-AI-Hackathon-Deck.pdf` in your browser or PDF viewer in full-screen presentation mode (`Ctrl + L` or `F11`).
3. **Open Live Application**:
   * Keep a tab open to `http://localhost:3000` for the dashboard and `http://localhost:3000/api-flow/NOVA-1001` for the holographic canvas.
4. **Follow the Script**:
   * Use [`Presenter-Script.md`](./Presenter-Script.md) for word-for-word spoken talking points and pacing cues.
5. **Consult Q&A during Judging**:
   * Keep [`Hackathon-Judge-QA.md`](./Hackathon-Judge-QA.md) handy on a secondary device or notes pane for instant answers to tough judge questions.
