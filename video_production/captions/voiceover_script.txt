## JiraPilot AI — 60-Second Product Video: Voice-Over Script
## Professional, clear, natural delivery. Calm, confident pace.
## Total duration: 60 seconds

---

### SCENE 1  [0:00–0:05]  — The Problem

"Every software incident starts with a question: where do we begin?"

Delivery notes:
  - Slow, deliberate pace. Slight pause after "question:".
  - Tone: thoughtful, empathetic — not alarming.

---

### SCENE 2  [0:05–0:12]  — Jira Issue Created

"JiraPilot AI turns a new Jira issue into a structured investigation."

Delivery notes:
  - Confident, clear. Emphasize "structured investigation."
  - Pick up pace slightly from Scene 1.

---

### SCENE 3  [0:12–0:20]  — Ticket Readiness

"It checks ticket readiness and helps developers request the details they need."

Delivery notes:
  - Measured, practical. "helps developers" is the key phrase.
  - No urgency — this is a calm, efficient process.

---

### SCENE 4  [0:20–0:30]  — AI Log Analysis

"It analyzes logs, connects related errors, and surfaces evidence behind likely failure points."

Delivery notes:
  - Slightly faster — more technical content.
  - Stress "connects" and "evidence."
  - Short natural pause between "errors" and "and surfaces."

---

### SCENE 5  [0:30–0:38]  — Repository Discovery

"It recommends approved repositories and prepares copy-ready clone commands for debugging."

Delivery notes:
  - Practical and crisp. "approved repositories" matters — enterprise credibility.
  - Smile slightly in voice on "copy-ready."

---

### SCENE 6  [0:38–0:47]  — Jira Comments & Email

"It prepares readable investigation reports and keeps the team informed."

Delivery notes:
  - Warm, collaborative. "keeps the team informed" lands best with a brief pause before it.

---

### SCENE 7  [0:47–0:54]  — Unified Workspace

"Evidence, next steps and investigation status stay in one place."

Delivery notes:
  - Clean and direct. No filler. Single breath.

---

### SCENE 8  [0:54–1:00]  — Closing Brand Reveal

"Less repetitive triage. Clearer investigations. More time for developers to solve problems."

Delivery notes:
  - Deliberate, stately. One beat between each sentence.
  - Fade voice naturally into silence before the 60-second mark.
  - Do NOT rush — allow space for the final visual reveal.

---

## TOTAL VO WORD COUNT: ~95 words
## TARGET SPEAKING RATE: ~95–100 WPM (unhurried, professional)
## RECOMMENDED VOICE STYLE: Warm baritone or clear mezzo, North American or neutral British accent
